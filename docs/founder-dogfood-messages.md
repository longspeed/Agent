# Founder dogfood message set

These are starting points for the founder's own Gmail queue. They are not a
bulk sequence and are not proof of performance. Review every address and draft,
personalize the one relevant sentence, and send only when the recipient and
reason are clear.

The point of the message is to find people who already feel the missed-reply
problem, not to sell warmup, rotation, inbox placement, or a replacement CRM.
Keep the daily run inside the configured cap and record the outcome in
Outreach's **Founder / agency dogfood log**.

## Agency operator

**Subject:** Client inbox follow-ups

Hi [First name] — quick question: when your team sends from several client
Gmail inboxes, who owns the replies and promised follow-ups that land after the
campaign is running?

I built Sendkeep as the memory layer for Gmail outreach: it watches the Sent
threads your team already created, catches promised actions, and keeps one
follow-up visible. It does not replace Gmail replies or add warmup and rotation.

Is that a problem worth comparing notes on, or should I close the loop?

## When someone replies

Ask one question at a time:

- How many Gmail inboxes does your team actively manage?
- What happens today when a prospect promises something after the first sender
  is busy?
- Is the missed work a promised follow-up, a date, or knowing whether a meeting
  actually happened?
- Would you run the memory layer on a second managed inbox if the first one
  saved time?

Do not turn a reply into a meeting or deal in public notes. Confirm outcomes in
the queue, and keep any quote or result private until the participant approves
its use.
