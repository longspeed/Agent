Data Processing

Last updated 23 July 2026

There is no executed DPA published here, on purpose. A Data Processing Agreement is a contract signed between two parties under Article 28 GDPR, not a notice a company posts on its website. Publishing a generated one would give you a document that looks binding and is not. If you need a DPA in place, request one at support@agenthub.app and it will be handled as a signed agreement.

What follows is the factual information a DPA would annex, and it is accurate today. Use it for your vendor review.

Roles

For the personal data of the people you contact, you are the controller and Agent Hub is the processor, acting on your documented instructions. You decide who is in your list and why. For your own account data, Agent Hub is the controller.

Subject matter and duration

Processing lasts for as long as your account exists. Subject matter is the sending of outreach email you have approved, the detection of replies to it, and optionally the discovery of prospective contacts.

Categories of data subject

  • You and any colleagues using your account
  • The prospects in your lead sheet
  • Anyone who replies to mail you sent

Categories of personal data

  • Names, business email addresses, company names
  • The content of outreach messages sent and replies received
  • For your account: email address, encrypted Google token, settings, usage counters

Agent Hub is not intended for special-category data. Do not put it in your sheet.

Point to raise in your review: reply drafting sends the message your prospect wrote to a language model provider. If that is unacceptable for your data, do not use reply drafting. Outreach sending and the lead sheet work without it.

Model tiers and training rights

Your reviewer will want this in writing, so it is here rather than in a footnote. The language model providers above are used on their free tiers, and a free tier generally reserves the right to use prompts and completions for model training. That is a term of the upstream provider's service, not something we can waive on their behalf.

What we can do is control what reaches such an endpoint, and we do:

  • First-touch outreach generation may run on a free tier. Its request carries the lead's name, sometimes a company, one note obtained from public web search, and the controller's own description of their offering. It contains no content authored by the data subject.
  • Reply drafting carries content authored by the data subject. It is routed only to endpoints that are not on a free tier whenever one is configured, and it is not failed over to a free-tier endpoint if those are unavailable — the draft fails instead.
  • Where no non-free endpoint is configured for a deployment, reply drafting will use a free tier and the training exposure applies. A controller who needs this excluded should ask for confirmation of the configured endpoints before relying on reply drafting; the setting that refuses free-tier reply drafting outright is REPLY_DRAFTS_REQUIRE_PRIVATE_ENDPOINT.

Security measures

Summarised on the security page: Google OAuth-only sign-in (no password), encrypted Google tokens at rest, signed HttpOnly session cookies, row-level security for tenant isolation, and rate limits on sending.

Data subject requests

Because your lead data lives in your own Google Sheet, most access, correction, and erasure requests are actioned by you directly in the sheet. For data held by Agent Hub, email support@agenthub.app. Note that self-service account deletion is not built yet and is handled manually.
