Sendkeep

Security

Last updated 20 August 2026

Written from the codebase, not from a certification. Sendkeep is early-stage software. This page states what is actually implemented, including the gaps. It is not a SOC 2 report and does not claim to be one.

Accounts and sessions

  • Sign-in is Google OAuth only — Sign in with Google, using an identity-only scope, separate from the Gmail and Sheets permissions. There is no password: nothing to guess, phish, or leak.
  • Sessions are a signed token in an HttpOnly cookie with SameSite=lax, marked Secure in production, expiring after 7 days. The signature is HMAC-SHA256, so the account id inside it cannot be altered.

Your Google connection

  • The OAuth token is encrypted before it is stored (Fernet, AES) and is never written to disk in plain text.
  • The OAuth state parameter is signed and short-lived, so an attacker cannot attach their Google account to your session.
  • Disconnecting from Settings erases the stored token immediately.
  • Google access is incremental. Monitoring requests Gmail read-only access. Gmail sending is requested only when you enable sending. Sheets and Drive metadata are a separate optional grant. Existing legacy tokens may retain gmail.modify until the account reconnects.

Tenant isolation

Every table carries an account id, every query filters on it, and row-level security is enabled on top. Background jobs are visible only to the account that started them. A row index is re-verified against the expected email address before any status is written, so a sheet edited mid-run cannot cause a write against the wrong person.

Guardrails on sending

  • Manual review is required in the standard deployment. Automatic first-touch delivery is disabled unless a deployment owner explicitly enables it for a controlled pilot. Replies and follow-ups always wait for individual review.
  • A row is only eligible when you have approved it by clearing its Status.
  • Sending is capped per account per 24 hours, and to 5 batches per hour.
  • Sendkeep refuses to write into your sheet at all until row 1 carries the full nine-column header. That is your explicit consent that those columns belong to the app rather than to your own notes.
  • No reply is sent to a prospect until you have read and approved it.

The approval contract

In plain language, Sendkeep's approval step is a safety gate, not a deliverability guarantee. Before a Sendkeep send, the server re-checks the account cap, recipient eligibility, suppression state, duplicate-send log, and bounce pause. It records the approved copy, timestamp, and Gmail thread after Google accepts the message.

Your click is still the final authorization. Review the recipient and copy before sending. Sendkeep cannot govern messages sent directly in Gmail or by another tool, cannot undo a human-approved mistake, and cannot promise inbox placement, domain reputation, warmup, or rotation.

Domain-reputation boundaries

Sendkeep is designed for thoughtful, low-volume sending from the Gmail inbox you already use — not for scaling around provider limits. Optional first-touch sending is capped on purpose and paced; replies and follow-ups always stay in manual review. Opt-outs are suppressed, hard-bounce signals can pause sending, and the queue shows the current cap and bounce state. If you need warmup, rotation, or inbox-placement management, that is a different product category and Sendkeep does not claim to provide it.

For optional first-touch sending, missing SPF or DMARC, a revoked DKIM key, or an unavailable DNS check pauses the send path. Reply monitoring still works while you fix the domain. A DNS check is not a deliverability promise; it is the minimum authentication gate before Sendkeep adds another message to your domain's sending history.

Your data and exit

Settings includes a JSON export of Sendkeep's account, queue, tracked threads, commitments, audit events, and usage records. OAuth credentials and session secrets are excluded. Disconnecting Gmail erases the stored token and stops monitoring and Sendkeep sends; mail already in Gmail stays in Gmail. Self-service account deletion is not implemented yet — request it at support@sendkeep.app.

Known gaps

Stated openly, because a security page that lists only strengths is not worth reading:

  • Self-service account deletion is not implemented yet; export first, then email support for deletion.
  • Reply drafting sends prospect message text to a third-party language model provider. See the privacy policy.
  • Sendkeep has not completed Google's OAuth verification review, so Gmail access is currently limited to allow-listed test accounts.

Reporting a vulnerability

Email support@sendkeep.app with enough detail to reproduce it. We will acknowledge, and we will not pursue anyone who reports in good faith and does not access other people's data.
