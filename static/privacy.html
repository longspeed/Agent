Agent Hub sends outreach email from your own Gmail account and tracks replies in your own Google Sheet. This policy explains what we store, what we send to other companies, and what we never do.

Two groups of people appear in this policy. You are the Agent Hub customer. Your prospects are the people you email. For your prospects' data, you are the controller and Agent Hub is a processor acting on your instructions.

Everything is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL). Every table is scoped by account and protected with row-level security, so one customer's queries cannot return another customer's rows.

What we do not store

  • Your lead list. It stays in your Google Sheet. We read it when a job runs and write status back. We keep no copy.
  • Your mailbox. We read only the threads Agent Hub itself started, to detect replies. We do not scan the rest of your inbox.
  • Payment card details. Billing is not implemented yet. When it is, a payment provider will handle card data and we will not see it.

Free model tiers and training

We use the free tiers of the providers above. This is the part people usually are not told, so here it is plainly: a free tier generally allows the provider to use what it is sent to improve their models. OpenRouter says prompts and completions on its free endpoints may be used for model improvement by the upstream provider, and Google says free-tier AI Studio data may be used to improve its products.

So we split what goes where:

  • Writing a first outreach email may use a free tier. What that request contains is the lead's name, sometimes their company, one note sourced from public web search, and your own description of what you are offering. Nothing your prospect wrote, and nothing they told you privately.
  • Drafting a reply contains the prospect's own words, so it is routed to a paid endpoint whenever one is configured, and it is not silently failed over to a free one if that endpoint is down. Where no paid endpoint is configured, reply drafting runs on a free tier and the training exposure above applies to your prospect's message. If that matters to you, ask us which endpoint your account is on before you use reply drafting — and if the answer is not one you can accept, do not use it.

We do not sell data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. That is a statement about what we do; it is not a promise about a free-tier provider's training rights, which is why the paragraphs above exist.

Your prospects' data

You choose who goes in your sheet. Agent Hub emails only rows you have approved, and drafts only replies you read before sending. If a prospect asks you to stop contacting them, honour it: remove or mark the row in your sheet. Agent Hub does not currently maintain a central suppression list, so that step is yours.

Retention and deletion

Account data is kept while your account exists. You can disconnect Google at any time from Settings, which erases the stored token immediately.

Self-service account deletion is not built yet. To have your account and its data erased, email support@agenthub.app and we will do it manually. We are stating this plainly rather than implying a button that does not exist.

Security

Sessions use a signed, HttpOnly cookie that expires after 14 days. Google tokens are encrypted before storage. Sign-in and sign-up are rate limited. Outbound sending is capped per account per 24 hours. More detail is on the security page.
