Agent Hub sends mail from your address, so misuse damages your domain reputation first and ours second. This page is the short version of what the product is for.

What Agent Hub is for

Sending a small volume of individually relevant, human-approved messages to people who have a plausible reason to hear from you, and replying to them like a person.

Not permitted

  • Bulk unsolicited mail. Scraped or purchased lists blasted without regard to fit. The daily cap makes this impractical by design; do not work around it by rotating accounts.
  • Ignoring an opt-out. If someone asks you to stop, stop. Mark or remove their row so they are never queued again.
  • Misrepresentation. Forged sender identity, false claims about who you are or who you represent, deceptive subject lines.
  • Illegal, harassing, or deceptive content, including phishing, malware links, and fraud.
  • Regulated-data outreach where you have no lawful basis, for example health or financial data about the recipient.
  • Circumventing the guardrails: the send cap, the approval step, or the reply review queue.

Your legal obligations

You are the sender. Depending on where you and your recipients are, rules such as CAN-SPAM, GDPR, and PECR apply to you directly. In broad terms that means being honest about who you are, giving people a way to opt out and honouring it promptly, and having a lawful basis for contacting them.

Agent Hub does not add an unsubscribe link for you. It is not implemented yet. If your jurisdiction requires one, include it in your meeting purpose or message content and handle opt-outs manually until the feature exists. We would rather tell you than let you assume it is handled.

Enforcement

If we see activity that looks like bulk unsolicited mail or generates spam complaints, we may suspend sending on the account. Where the risk is immediate we may act first and explain after. Contact support@agenthub.app to dispute.
