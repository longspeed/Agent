The approval contract

Your click is the final authorization. Here is exactly what Sendkeep checks, and what it cannot promise.

Full security details →
What approval protects
  • • Optional first-touch drafts stay in More until you press Send.
  • • The server re-checks recipient eligibility, opt-outs, duplicate history, daily cap, and bounce pause at send time.
  • • Paid inboxes are capped at 50 optional first-touch sends per rolling 24 hours; replies and follow-ups never auto-send.
  • • The core promise workflow is read-only; Gmail or Gemini remains the reply surface.
  • • The approved copy, timestamp, and Gmail thread are recorded after Google accepts the send.
What approval cannot protect
  • • A human can still approve the wrong recipient or copy, so review both before sending.
  • • Gmail or the worker can be delayed; check the monitoring state before trusting stale coverage.
  • • Messages sent directly in Gmail or by another tool are outside Sendkeep's controls.
  • • No warmup, rotation, domain-reputation, or inbox-placement guarantee is implied.

How the promise desk works

Sendkeep watches recent Gmail Sent threads, extracts promised actions, and creates at most one follow-up reminder per contact. Gmail stays your reply surface.

When you press Prepare drafts

  1. Checks your sheet's header row

    All nine column labels have to be in row 1 first. Until they are, the agent writes nothing at all, so it can never overwrite data you keep in unlabeled columns.

  2. Picks who is eligible

    Only rows that have an email address and a blank Status, and that nobody has unsubscribed. Anything already marked Sent, Replied, Bounced, Unsubscribed or Needs verification is left alone.

  3. Applies the daily cap

    At most 25 emails from your inbox in any 24 hours. Contacts over the cap wait for the next batch instead of being dropped.

  4. Writes and checks each email

    A language model drafts a subject line and a short email per contact, using your sender name, what you're reaching out about, your call-to-action link (if you set one), and the contact's research note. Each draft is validated (no placeholders, no spam tells, link present when you have one) before it's queued.

  5. Queues them for review by default

    With manual review enabled, every draft lands in "Drafts awaiting approval" below. Nothing is emailed until you read and approve it. A separately gated controlled deployment may automatically deliver optional first-touch drafts after confirmation; replies and follow-ups always wait for review.

When you approve a draft

  1. Sends from your own Gmail

    The email goes out from the Google account you connected, as a normal message on a normal thread, with a one-click unsubscribe link and List-Unsubscribe headers.

  2. Marks the row the moment it sends

    Status, thread ID, timestamp and the exact text that was sent, written immediately and retried three times. That is what stops anyone being emailed twice.

  3. Re-checks the cap and opt-outs at send time

    Right before each send, it confirms you're under the daily cap and the recipient hasn't unsubscribed since the draft was made. "Send all" does this per email and paces them apart.

After the emails go out

  1. Watches for replies

    While this page is open it re-checks every sent thread about once every two minutes, looking for a new message from the person you contacted.

  2. Drafts your response

    When someone writes back, the model reads the whole conversation and drafts an answer to what they actually said. The row is marked Replied.

  3. Waits for you to approve it

    The draft lands in "Replies to review" below. You can edit every word, and nothing is sent until you press Send reply.
